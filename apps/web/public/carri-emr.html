Core Capabilities

Designed for everyday clinical care.

Carri EMR supports the day-to-day workflows of healthcare facilities, helping clinicians spend less time searching for information and more time delivering care.

01Patient Identity

Carri ID: Every patient receives a unique Carri ID and physical Carri ID Card, creating a trusted identifier for healthcare information across participating healthcare facilities.

02Health Records

Electronic Medical Records: Capture and manage complete digital health records throughout the patient's healthcare journey.

Longitudinal Health History: Access a patient's healthcare history across participating healthcare facilities to support continuity of care.

03Health Information Exchange

Healthcare Information Retrieval: Securely retrieve relevant healthcare information when patients present their Carri ID at another participating healthcare facility.

Continuity of Care: Support referrals and coordinated care with trusted healthcare information available at the point of care.

04Clinical Care

Clinical Documentation: Document consultations, diagnoses, procedures, and treatment plans digitally.

Laboratory Management: Capture and review laboratory requests and results within the patient's health record.

Pharmacy & Prescriptions: Manage prescriptions and medication history electronically.

Referral: Support coordinated referrals between participating healthcare facilities.

05Privacy & Security

Patient Consent: Healthcare information is shared only with appropriate patient consent and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

Role-Based Access: Only authorised healthcare professionals can access healthcare information based on their responsibilities.

Audit Trails: Every access and activity is recorded to support accountability and transparency.

06Healthcare Intelligence

Disease Surveillance: Responsibly anonymized healthcare information helps identify disease trends and emerging public health concerns.

Population Health: Generate insights that help healthcare leaders understand the health needs of communities.

Health System Planning: Support evidence-based planning and resource allocation through trusted healthcare intelligence.

Built for trust.

Healthcare information is protected through patient consent, role-based access controls, audit trails, encryption, and robust security measures.

Only authorised healthcare professionals within participating healthcare facilities can access healthcare information for legitimate care purposes.
