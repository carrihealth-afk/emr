Carri Intelligence responsibly transforms anonymized healthcare information generated through the Carri ecosystem into actionable intelligence for healthcare planning, disease surveillance, and public health decision-making.

Core Capabilities

01Disease Surveillance

Monitor disease trends and identify emerging public health concerns through real-time healthcare intelligence.

02Population Health Intelligence

Understand the health needs of communities with insights into disease burden, demographics, and healthcare utilisation.

03Health System Planning

Support evidence-based planning with insights that help inform healthcare investments, workforce planning, and service delivery.

04Resource Allocation

Identify where healthcare resources are needed most to improve efficiency and strengthen healthcare delivery.

05Performance & Reporting

Generate dashboards and reports that help healthcare organisations and policymakers monitor health system performance.

06Health Trends & Forecasting

Identify emerging healthcare trends to support proactive planning and timely public health interventions.

Built on Responsible Data Use

Carri Intelligence uses responsibly anonymized healthcare information.

No personally identifiable information is used to generate healthcare intelligence, ensuring patient privacy while enabling meaningful insights that benefit healthcare systems.
