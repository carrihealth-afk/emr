Healthcare is becoming increasingly connected

Patients receive care across multiple healthcare facilities, clinics, and community settings.

The increasing volume and diversity of healthcare information means clinicians and healthcare leaders need timely access to that information to make effective decisions and improve outcomes.

As healthcare evolves, the role of healthcare information is evolving with it.

Information that supports every stage of care

Healthcare information is no longer just a record of past events. It is a vital resource that supports diagnosis, treatment, referrals, continuity of care, and long-term health planning.

When trusted healthcare information is available at the point of care, healthcare professionals can make more informed decisions based on a more complete understanding of a patient’s healthcare journey.

Beyond the point of care

The value of healthcare information extends beyond individual patient care.

Responsibly anonymized healthcare information can help identify disease trends, support public health planning, improve resource allocation, and provide healthcare leaders with the insights needed to strengthen health systems.

This enables decisions that are informed by evidence and responsive to the changing healthcare needs of communities.

Looking ahead

The future of healthcare will depend not only on the quality of care delivered, but also on the quality and availability of the information that supports it.

Creating stronger health systems begins with ensuring trusted healthcare information is available when it is needed, protected by robust privacy safeguards, and used responsibly to improve care and inform better decisions.

Healthcare information has always been valuable. The opportunity now is to ensure it works harder for patients, healthcare professionals, and the health systems that serve them.
