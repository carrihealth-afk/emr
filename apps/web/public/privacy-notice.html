1. Introduction

At Carri Health, we believe that protecting personal information is fundamental to delivering reliable, secure, and responsible healthcare services.

Carri Health is a digital health records and data intelligence platform designed to improve healthcare delivery through Electronic Medical Records (EMR), patient identification, secure health information management, and healthcare data analytics.

This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, use our platform, interact with our services, or engage with us in any other capacity.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly, maintaining confidentiality, and respecting the privacy rights of patients, healthcare professionals, healthcare facilities, and other individuals who interact with our services.

Our processing of personal information is subject to applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, where applicable.

Where consent is the required legal basis for processing, we will obtain it in accordance with applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Carri Health, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Personal Identification Information

This may include:

  • Full name.
  • Date of birth.
  • Gender, where relevant and lawfully collected.
  • Phone number.
  • Email address.
  • Residential or contact address.
  • Patient identification details.
  • Account registration information.

2.2 Health and Medical Information

When our services are used to manage patient records, we may process health information such as:

  • Medical history.
  • Diagnoses and clinical notes.
  • Medications and prescriptions.
  • Laboratory results and diagnostic reports.
  • Allergies and relevant medical conditions.
  • Treatment records and care plans.
  • Referrals and appointment information.
  • Other relevant clinical information entered by authorized healthcare providers.

Health information is sensitive personal data. We apply appropriate safeguards and process it only for legitimate purposes supported by a valid legal basis.

2.3 Healthcare Professional and Facility Information

For healthcare professionals and participating facilities, we may collect:

  • Names and professional contact details.
  • Professional registration or identification information, where required.
  • Facility names, addresses, and contact details.
  • Staff roles and access permissions.
  • Account activity and service usage information.
  • Relevant organizational and operational information.

2.4 Technical and Usage Information

When you visit our website or use our digital services, we may collect:

  • IP address and device information.
  • Browser type and operating system.
  • Login records and access timestamps.
  • Platform usage and navigation information.
  • Security logs and diagnostic information.
  • Network and server connection information.
  • Cookie and similar tracking information, where applicable.

This information helps us maintain platform security, enforce access restrictions, troubleshoot technical problems, and improve the reliability of our services.

2.5 Communications and Support Information

If you contact us, we may collect your name, contact details, messages, enquiries, feedback, and other information you voluntarily provide.

We use this information to respond to requests, resolve issues, and improve our services.

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect personal information through:

Direct interactions: When you register, complete a form, contact us, or provide information directly.

Healthcare facilities: When an authorized healthcare facility or professional enters or submits information in connection with healthcare services.

Platform usage: Through account activity, system logs, and technical interactions with our website or applications.

Server and network connections: Through information generated when authorized users access Carri Health services within accredited healthcare facilities.

Authorized integrations: Through connected healthcare systems or service providers, where appropriate and legally permitted.

Other lawful sources: Where information is obtained through a legally permitted process and is necessary for our services.

Where appropriate, we will provide additional information about the collection and use of personal data at the point of collection.

4. Why We Use Your Information

Carri Health processes personal information for specific, legitimate, and lawful purposes, including the following.

4.1 Delivering Digital Healthcare Services

We use relevant information to provide electronic medical records, support patient identification, manage appointments, organize clinical documentation, and enable authorized retrieval of health information.

4.2 Supporting Continuity of Patient Care

Carri Health aims to help authorized healthcare providers access relevant patient information across participating healthcare facilities.

This can reduce unnecessary duplication of records and help clinicians make better-informed decisions.

Access to identifiable patient information remains subject to appropriate permissions, applicable law, and the relevant legal basis for processing.

4.3 Enforcing Facility-Based Access Restrictions

Carri Health services are designed to be accessed exclusively within the premises of healthcare facilities accredited and authorized to use the platform, through the Carri Health server.

We may process relevant technical, account, network, and access-log information to:

Authenticate authorized users. Verify access through the designated Carri Health server. Support the enforcement of facility-based access restrictions. Prevent unauthorized access from outside permitted facility environments.

Monitor suspicious activity and investigate potential security incidents.

Protect the confidentiality and integrity of patient health records.

Access is subject to authentication procedures, user permissions, applicable security controls, and the terms of the relevant facility agreement.

Attempts to bypass these restrictions or gain unauthorized access to the platform are prohibited.

4.4 Improving Healthcare Delivery

We may use appropriate operational information to improve platform functionality, reliability, usability, and service delivery.

4.5 Health Data Intelligence and Analytics

We may analyze appropriately collected health data to generate aggregated or de-identified insights that support:

  • Public health planning.
  • Healthcare service evaluation.
  • Health research and innovation.
  • Resource allocation.
  • Disease surveillance, where legally authorized.
  • Evidence-based healthcare interventions.
  • Understanding healthcare utilization and operational trends.

Where information can identify an individual, we will apply the relevant legal requirements and safeguards before using or sharing it for these purposes.

4.6 Healthcare Business Analytics

We may process relevant facility and operational data to provide insights into healthcare facility activities, service utilization, appointment trends, and other operational performance indicators.

4.7 Communication and Customer Support

We use contact information to respond to enquiries, provide service-related notifications, communicate important updates, and offer technical support.

Marketing communications, where applicable, will be handled in accordance with relevant legal requirements and your communication preferences.

4.8 Security, Fraud Prevention, and Legal Compliance

We may process relevant information to authenticate users, manage account access, detect suspicious activity, protect health records, prevent fraud, respond to lawful requests, and meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Carri Health processes personal information only where an appropriate legal basis exists under applicable data protection law.

Depending on the circumstances, these legal bases may include:

Consent: Where an individual has freely and appropriately consented to a specified purpose.

Contract: Where processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with the individual.

Legal obligation: Where processing is required to comply with applicable law.

Vital interests: Where processing is necessary to protect someone's vital interests in circumstances recognized by law.

Public interest: Where processing is authorized or required for a lawful public-interest purpose.

Legitimate interests: Where applicable, where a legitimate interest provides a lawful basis and the individual's rights and interests are appropriately considered.

Because medical information requires heightened protection, processing health data will also be subject to any additional conditions and safeguards required by law.

Consent will not be treated as the legal basis for every processing activity where another lawful basis appropriately applies.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent, subject to applicable legal requirements and the consequences explained when consent is obtained.

6. How We Share Information

Carri Health does not treat personal information as information that may be freely disclosed.

We may share information in the following circumstances, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

6.1 Authorized Healthcare Facilities and Professionals

Relevant patient information may be made available to authorized healthcare professionals or participating facilities where necessary for legitimate healthcare purposes and supported by a valid legal basis.

Access is subject to applicable permissions, confidentiality requirements, and appropriate security controls.

6.2 Technology and Service Providers

We may engage trusted third-party service providers to support hosting, server infrastructure, technical maintenance, cybersecurity, communications, and other essential operations.

Such providers may process information on our behalf under appropriate contractual, security, and confidentiality arrangements.

6.3 Research and Public Health

We may share aggregated or appropriately de-identified information for legitimate research, public health planning, and healthcare improvement where permitted by law.

Identifiable health information will not be disclosed for these purposes unless the disclosure has a valid legal basis and satisfies applicable requirements.

6.4 Legal and Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose information where required by law, a valid legal order, or an applicable regulatory obligation.

6.5 Business Transactions

If Carri Health undergoes a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or similar business transaction, relevant information may be transferred where legally permitted and subject to appropriate privacy and security safeguards.

We do not sell personal health information as a commercial product.

7. Carri Health ID and Cross-Facility Records

Carri Health ID is designed to support patient identification and continuity of care across participating healthcare facilities.

Depending on the services available and the applicable permissions, authorized healthcare providers may be able to retrieve relevant patient information from records maintained through the platform.

However:

Possession of a Carri Health ID does not automatically authorize unrestricted access to medical records. Access must be limited to authorized purposes. Healthcare providers must comply with applicable confidentiality and data protection obligations. Information sharing must have an appropriate legal basis. Patients' applicable privacy rights must be respected.

Carri Health aims to make relevant health information available when needed while maintaining appropriate safeguards for patient confidentiality.

7.1 Access to Carri Health Services Through Accredited Facilities

Carri Health services are designed to be accessed exclusively within the premises of healthcare facilities accredited and authorized to use the platform, through the Carri Health server.

Access is restricted to authorized users operating within an accredited facility's premises and is subject to authentication procedures, role-based permissions, and applicable security protocols.

This facility-based access model is intended to strengthen the security and confidentiality of patient health information, promote responsible data management, and ensure that medical records are accessed within an authorized healthcare environment.

Users must not attempt to access Carri Health services outside the permitted facility environment, bypass the designated server, circumvent access restrictions, or obtain unauthorized access to patient records.

Carri Health may restrict or suspend access where unauthorized activity, security risks, or violations of these requirements are identified, subject to applicable law and contractual obligations.

Technical information relating to server connections, access attempts, authentication, and security logs may be processed to enforce these restrictions and protect the platform.

Facility-based access does not automatically authorize access to every patient's record. Users must have the appropriate permissions and a valid reason to access specific information.

8. Health Data Intelligence, Anonymization, and Research

Health data intelligence is an important part of our mission to support better healthcare decisions.

Where appropriate, Carri Health may use aggregated or de-identified information to identify trends, evaluate service delivery, and support public health planning and research.

We seek to minimize the use of identifiable information for these purposes.

Where data is described as anonymized, reasonable measures should have been taken to prevent the identification of individuals. Information that remains identifiable, including through combination with other available information, continues to require appropriate protection.

We will not label information as anonymized to avoid legal obligations where individuals can still reasonably be identified.

Any use of identifiable health data for research or other secondary purposes will be subject to the applicable legal basis, consent requirements where relevant, and any other required approvals or safeguards.

9. Data Security

Carri Health recognizes the importance of protecting personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, and destruction.

We aim to implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, which may include:

  • Access controls and role-based permissions.
  • Authentication and account security measures.
  • Encryption where appropriate.
  • Secure server and network configurations.
  • Monitoring of access attempts and security logs.
  • Secure data storage and transmission practices.
  • Staff confidentiality obligations.
  • Backup and recovery procedures.
  • Procedures for responding to suspected security incidents.

No digital system can guarantee absolute security. We therefore continue to review and improve our safeguards as our services and associated risks evolve.

Where a personal data breach occurs, we will take appropriate steps to investigate, mitigate, and report the incident in accordance with applicable law.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, the purpose of processing, the relevant healthcare requirements, and applicable law.

Medical records may be subject to specific retention obligations. Accordingly, a request to delete personal information may not always result in immediate or complete deletion where continued retention is legally required or otherwise lawfully justified.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete, securely dispose of, or irreversibly anonymize it, as appropriate and legally permitted.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

Be informed: Understand how and why your personal information is processed.

Access: Request access to personal information we hold about you.

Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Erasure: Request deletion of personal information where the applicable legal conditions are met.

Restriction: Request that certain processing activities be restricted where permitted by law.

Object: Object to certain types of processing where the law provides this right.

Data portability: Request a copy of qualifying information in an appropriate format where the right applies.

Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Automated decision-making protections: Exercise applicable rights concerning decisions made solely through automated processing.

Lodge a complaint: Submit a complaint to the relevant data protection authority.

These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. For example, certain medical records may need to be retained to satisfy legal or healthcare obligations.

To exercise your rights, contact Carri Health using the contact details provided in this Notice. We may need to verify your identity before responding to protect your information and prevent unauthorized disclosure.

Where a request concerns records maintained by a healthcare facility, we may need to coordinate with the relevant facility or direct you to the appropriate record custodian.

12. Children's Privacy

Carri Health recognizes that children's personal and medical information requires particular care.

Where our services involve children, their information will be processed in accordance with applicable law, including relevant requirements concerning parental or guardian authorization, consent, and the best interests of the child.

We will take appropriate steps to ensure that access to children's records is restricted to authorized persons and legitimate purposes.

13. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential functionality, maintain security, remember preferences, and understand website performance.

Where required, we will provide appropriate information and obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

You may be able to manage cookie preferences through your browser or the relevant website settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functions.

We will provide additional details about specific cookies and their purposes where applicable.

14. International Data Transfers

Carri Health may use service providers or technology infrastructure located outside Nigeria.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take the steps required under applicable law to ensure that the transfer has a valid legal basis and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

These safeguards may include relevant contractual protections and other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

Further information about applicable transfers may be obtained by contacting us.

15. Third-Party Websites and Integrations

Our website or platform may contain links to third-party websites or integrate with external services.

Those third parties may operate under their own privacy notices and terms of use.

Carri Health does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services and encourages users to review their policies before sharing personal information with them.

This does not affect any obligations Carri Health may have under applicable law in relation to its own processing activities.

16. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice as our services, technology, legal obligations, or data processing practices change.

The updated version will be published on our website with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before implementing changes that require it. We encourage you to revisit this notice periodically.

18. Our Commitment

At Carri Health, protecting personal information is central to building a trusted digital healthcare ecosystem.

We are committed to responsible health information management, appropriate data security and the lawful use of health data to support continuity of care, healthcare innovation, and better - informed public health decisions.

Our facility - based access model, authorized server connections, and access controls are intended to help protect patient confidentiality while enabling authorized healthcare professionals to access relevant information within participating healthcare facilities.

Our goal is to connect health records, empower healthcare providers, and support better care while respecting every individual’s right to privacy.
