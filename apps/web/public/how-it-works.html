How It Works

A trusted way to make
healthcare information
available at the point of
care.

Carri Health enables participating healthcare facilities to securely access trusted healthcare information through a unique patient identifier.

The Carri Ecosystem

01Healthcare facilities join the Carri ecosystem.

Healthcare facilities are onboarded into the Carri ecosystem and begin using Carri EMR as part of their clinical workflow.

02Every patient receives a Carri ID.

When a patient visits a participating healthcare facility for the first time, they are registered and issued a unique Carri ID and a physical Carri ID Card.

The Carri ID becomes the trusted identifier for the patient's healthcare information across the Carri ecosystem.

03Every healthcare encounter is linked to the Carri ID.

Every consultation, diagnosis, laboratory result, prescription, referral, admission, and other healthcare interactions recorded through Carri EMR are securely linked to the patient's Carri ID.

Over time, this creates a comprehensive longitudinal health history.

04Healthcare information is securely available where it is needed.

When a patient presents their Carri ID at another participating healthcare facility, authorised healthcare professionals can securely retrieve relevant healthcare information to support clinical decision-making and continuity of care.

Access is governed by patient consent, role-based access controls, and robust privacy and security measures, ensuring healthcare information is only available to authorised healthcare professionals for legitimate care purposes.

05Healthcare intelligence for stronger health systems.

Responsibly anonymized healthcare information is transformed through Carri Intelligence into actionable intelligence that supports:

  • Disease surveillance
  • Population health monitoring
  • Health system planning
  • Resource allocation
  • Public health decision-making

No personally identifiable information is used for healthcare intelligence.
