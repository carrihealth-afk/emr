About Carri Health

We believe better
healthcare outcomes
begin with better
information.

Every day, healthcare professionals make decisions that change lives. Those decisions depend on having the right information at the right time.

Today, every healthcare facility is a data island, leaving valuable healthcare information where it was first created.

That's why Carri Health exists.

To make trusted healthcare information available at the point of care, supporting better clinical decisions, stronger continuity of care, and improved healthcare outcomes.

The Challenge

Healthcare doesn't
happen in one place.

People receive care across hospitals, specialist clinics, and primary healthcare centres.

Yet the information created throughout that journey often remains fragmented and rarely follows them, even when they move between healthcare facilities or relocate to another city.

Without access to a complete health history, healthcare providers face repeated investigations, delayed referrals, gaps in continuity of care, and decisions made with incomplete information.

Beyond Care Delivery

Healthcare information has the power to do more than support individual care.

Carri Health believes that responsibly anonymized healthcare information can help strengthen health systems by turning everyday healthcare interactions into meaningful insights.

From disease surveillance and population health to resource planning and healthcare policy, these insights help healthcare leaders make more informed decisions for the communities they serve.

Our Vision

We envision a future where healthcare information is no longer confined to
the facilities where it was created, but follows the people it belongs to,
supporting better care, stronger health systems, and healthier
communities.
