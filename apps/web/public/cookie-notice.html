1. Introduction

At Carri Health, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how information is collected and used when you visit our website.

This Cookie Notice explains how Carri Health uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, maintain security, understand website usage, and enhance your browsing experience.

Carri Health is a digital health records and data intelligence platform focused on improving healthcare delivery through Electronic Medical Records (EMR), secure patient information management, and healthcare analytics.

This Notice applies to cookies and similar technologies used on our public website and, where applicable, our digital services.

It should be read alongside our Privacy Notice and Terms of Use.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember certain information about your visit, maintain sessions, improve performance, and provide essential functionality.

Similar technologies, such as local storage, tracking pixels, and software development tools, may also collect or store information on your device or browser.

Depending on their purpose, these technologies may collect information such as browser preferences, device details, session identifiers, and website usage information.

Cookies do not automatically provide access to the medical records stored within Carri Health's platform.

3. Why Carri Health Uses Cookies

Carri Health may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes.

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies support the essential operation and security of our website.

They may be used to:

Maintain secure website sessions. Support authentication and account security. Protect against fraudulent activity. Maintain system stability. Support security-related functions. Remember privacy preferences.

These cookies are necessary for the website or a service specifically requested by the user to function properly.

Where permitted by applicable law, strictly necessary cookies may be used without separate consent.

3.2 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies help improve your experience by remembering preferences and settings.

They may be used to:

Remember language preferences. Maintain selected website settings. Improve navigation. Support accessibility features.

Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before using these cookies.

3.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

If implemented, analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

They may provide information about:

Pages visited. Time spent on the website. Website navigation patterns. Technical errors and performance issues. General visitor interactions.

We use this information to evaluate website performance and improve our digital services.

Where required by applicable law, analytics cookies will only be activated after the appropriate consent has been obtained.

3.4 Security and Fraud Prevention Technologies

Carri Health may use cookies or similar technologies to help identify suspicious activity, protect user sessions, and maintain the security of its digital infrastructure.

These technologies support our efforts to prevent unauthorized access and protect personal information.

3.5 Marketing Cookies

If Carri Health introduces marketing or advertising technologies, these may be used to understand campaign effectiveness or provide relevant promotional content.

Such technologies will only be used where appropriate and in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including obtaining consent where required.

Carri Health does not claim to use advertising or marketing cookies unless these technologies have actually been implemented.

4. Cookies and Carri Health's Healthcare Platform

Carri Health provides digital healthcare services designed to support the secure management and retrieval of patient health information.

Our clinical platform is intended to be accessed within the premises of accredited healthcare facilities through the Carri Health server.

Cookies or similar technologies may be used, where implemented, to support authorized sessions, authentication, security, and system functionality.

However:

Cookies do not independently authorize access to patient medical records. Access to clinical information remains subject to authentication, user permissions, and applicable security controls. Users must not attempt to bypass access restrictions or access records without authorization. Sensitive medical information should not be stored in ordinary browser cookies.

Carri Health aims to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information through cookies and similar technologies.

5. Types of Cookies Based on Duration

Cookies may also be categorized according to how long they remain on your device.

Session cookies: These are temporary cookies that generally expire when you close your browser.

Persistent cookies: These remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them. They may help remember preferences or settings between visits.

The duration of any cookie depends on its purpose and configuration.

6. First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set directly by the Carri Health website.

Third-party cookies are set by external services integrated into our website, such as analytics providers or other technology partners, if such integrations are implemented.

Third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

Carri Health will take appropriate steps to assess relevant third-party technologies and ensure that their use complies with applicable data protection requirements.

Where required, your consent will be obtained before non-essential third-party cookies are activated.

7. Your Cookie Preferences

Where applicable, Carri Health may provide a cookie banner or preference management tool that allows you to accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookies.

You may be able to:

Accept all cookies. Reject non-essential cookies. Allow selected categories of cookies. Change your preferences later.

Strictly necessary cookies may remain active where they are required to provide a service you have requested or to meet another applicable legal condition.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

If a cookie preference management tool is provided, you can use it to review or update your choices.

8. Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies through their settings.

You may also configure your browser to notify you when cookies are being stored.

However, blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality, including session management, saved preferences, and other essential features.

Deleting a cookie does not necessarily delete other information that Carri Health or a healthcare facility may lawfully retain in its systems.

For this reason, browser cookie management should not be confused with a request to access, correct, or delete personal information held by Carri Health.

9. Personal Information and Data Protection

Information collected through cookies may constitute personal data when it identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual.

Where applicable, Carri Health processes such information in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other relevant data protection laws.

We aim to:

Use cookies for specified and legitimate purposes. Limit collection to information reasonably necessary for those purposes. Apply appropriate technical and organizational safeguards. Obtain consent where required. Respect applicable privacy rights. Avoid unnecessary collection of sensitive health information through website cookies.

For more information about how Carri Health collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information, please read our Privacy Notice.
