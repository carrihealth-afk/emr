1. Our Commitment to Service Excellence

At Carri Health, we understand that reliable access to health information is essential for efficient healthcare delivery and continuity of patient care.

Our platform is designed to help healthcare facilities manage electronic medical records, retrieve patient information, coordinate care, and access meaningful healthcare insights.

We are committed to providing reliable digital healthcare services, maintaining appropriate security measures, and supporting healthcare facilities in their day-to-day operations.

Our service levels outline our approach to platform availability, technical support, system maintenance, data security, and incident management.

2. Scope of Services

Our service levels apply to the Carri Health services made available to accredited healthcare facilities, including:

  • Electronic Medical Records (EMR): Digital creation, storage, management, and retrieval of patient records.
  • Carri Health ID: Patient identification and authorized access to relevant medical information across participating facilities.
  • Health Data Intelligence: Data-driven insights to support healthcare planning, research, and decision-making.
  • Healthcare Business Analytics: Operational insights that help facilities monitor activities and evaluate performance.
  • Technical Support: Assistance with platform access, troubleshooting, and technical issues.

Access to clinical Carri Health services is restricted to authorized users within the premises of accredited healthcare facilities through the Carri Health server, subject to the applicable access controls and service arrangements.

3. Platform Availability

Carri Health aims to maintain a reliable platform that supports the operational needs of participating healthcare facilities.

Our proposed service availability target is 99.5% per calendar month, subject to technical validation and the terms of the applicable service agreement.

Availability may be affected by scheduled maintenance, connectivity problems, power interruptions, third-party infrastructure failures, emergencies, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

Where reasonably practicable, we will notify affected facilities of planned maintenance that may interrupt access to services.

We will work to restore affected services as quickly as reasonably possible when unplanned interruptions occur.

4. Technical Support and Response Times

Carri Health aims to provide timely assistance based on the severity and operational impact of reported issues.

Our proposed support response targets are:

PriorityDescriptionTarget Initial Response

These are proposed initial response targets, not guaranteed resolution times. Actual support coverage and response commitments will depend on the applicable service agreement.

5. Incident Management

Carri Health recognizes that technical incidents can affect healthcare facility operations.

When an incident is reported, we aim to:

1. Receive and document the report. 2. Assess the severity and potential impact. 3. Investigate the underlying issue. 4. Prioritize incidents according to their operational and security implications. 5. Implement an appropriate fix or temporary workaround where possible. 6. Communicate relevant updates to affected facilities. 7. Review significant incidents and identify opportunities to prevent recurrence.

Incidents involving suspected unauthorized access to patient records or potential data breaches will be handled according to applicable security procedures and legal obligations.

6. Scheduled Maintenance and Updates

Regular maintenance helps preserve platform stability, security, and performance.

Carri Health may carry out maintenance to:

  • Improve system performance.
  • Deploy software updates.
  • Address security vulnerabilities.
  • Maintain server infrastructure.
  • Correct software defects.
  • Introduce new features and improvements.

Where maintenance may affect service availability, we aim to notify affected healthcare facilities in advance whenever reasonably practicable.

Emergency maintenance may be necessary to address critical security vulnerabilities, system failures, or other urgent technical risks.

7. Data Security and Confidentiality

Protecting patient health information is central to Carri Health's operations.

We aim to maintain appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including:

  • Authorized user authentication.
  • Role-based access controls.
  • Secure server and network configurations.
  • Appropriate protection of information during storage and transmission.
  • Security monitoring and access logging.
  • Procedures for identifying and responding to security incidents.

Only authorized users should access patient records, and access must be consistent with their assigned permissions and legitimate responsibilities.

Our facility-based access model is designed to ensure that clinical services are accessed within accredited healthcare facilities through the Carri Health server.

No digital system can guarantee absolute security. We continuously seek to improve our security practices in line with evolving risks and applicable requirements.

8. Data Backup and Recovery

Carri Health recognizes the importance of preserving health records and supporting recovery following technical failures.

Our data protection and recovery arrangements are intended to reduce the risk of permanent data loss and support the restoration of affected services.

Backup frequency, retention periods, recovery time objectives, and recovery point objectives will be established according to the deployed infrastructure and the applicable agreement with each healthcare facility.

Where a disruption occurs, we will make reasonable efforts to restore affected services and recover information in accordance with our established procedures.

Specific backup and recovery commitments will be communicated to participating facilities where applicable.

9. Performance and Continuous Improvement

We are committed to improving the reliability and usability of our services.

Our improvement efforts may include:

  • Monitoring platform performance.
  • Reviewing reported technical issues.
  • Identifying recurring service interruptions.
  • Improving system efficiency.
  • Updating security controls.
  • Gathering feedback from participating facilities.
  • Enhancing the usability of our EMR and analytics tools.

Feedback from healthcare professionals and facility administrators helps us identify opportunities to improve service delivery.

10. Responsibilities of Healthcare Facilities

Healthcare facilities using Carri Health are expected to:

  • Maintain suitable devices and local network infrastructure.
  • Provide reliable power and connectivity where required.
  • Ensure that only authorized personnel access the platform.
  • Keep user credentials confidential.
  • Assign appropriate access permissions to staff.
  • Report technical problems and suspected security incidents promptly.
  • Ensure that patient information entered into the platform is accurate.
  • Follow established procedures during service interruptions.
  • Maintain appropriate clinical documentation and patient care procedures.

Healthcare facilities remain responsible for clinical decisions and for complying with their professional, operational, and legal obligations.

11. Service Limitations

Although Carri Health aims to provide reliable and secure services, uninterrupted availability cannot be guaranteed.

Service interruptions may result from:

  • Internet or local network failures.
  • Power outages.
  • Hardware or software failures.
  • Scheduled maintenance.
  • Cybersecurity incidents.
  • Third-party service disruptions.
  • Events beyond our reasonable control.

Carri Health's responsibility for service availability, incident response, data recovery, and other service commitments will be governed by the applicable service agreement and relevant law.

Important: Carri Health is a technology platform designed to support healthcare delivery. It does not replace professional clinical judgment or the emergency procedures of healthcare facilities.

Healthcare facilities should maintain appropriate alternative procedures for accessing essential clinical information and documenting patient care during system downtime.

12. Service Reviews and Communication

Carri Health aims to maintain open communication with participating healthcare facilities concerning significant service issues, planned maintenance, and relevant platform improvements.

Where appropriate, we may provide updates concerning:

  • Service interruptions.
  • Maintenance schedules.
  • Incident resolution progress.
  • Platform upgrades.
  • Changes to supported features.
  • Relevant security and operational notices.

Material changes to agreed service commitments will be handled in accordance with the applicable service agreement.

13. Contact and Technical Support

For technical assistance, incident reporting, or questions about Carri Health services, please contact our support team.

Company: Carri Health Technical Support Email: Support@carrihealth.com Website: www.carrihealth.com Support Hours: 24hrs

When reporting a technical issue, please provide the name of your healthcare facility, a description of the problem, the time it occurred, and any relevant error message.

Do not include unnecessary patient-identifiable information in ordinary support emails.

For urgent clinical situations, healthcare professionals should follow their facility's established clinical escalation and emergency procedures rather than relying solely on technical support.

14. Review of Service Levels

Carri Health may periodically review these service levels to reflect improvements in our technology, infrastructure, support arrangements, and operational requirements.

Any binding service commitments will be specified in the applicable agreement with the healthcare facility.
