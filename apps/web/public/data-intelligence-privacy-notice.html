1. Introduction

At Carri Health, we recognise that health data is highly sensitive and must be handled with the highest standards of confidentiality, security and responsibility.

Our Data Intelligence service is designed to transform fragmented healthcare data into meaningful insights that support better healthcare delivery, evidence-based decision-making, medical research and public health development.

This Privacy Notice explains how Carri Health collects, processes, analyses, stores and protects health-related information used within its Data Intelligence services.

We are committed to protecting the privacy and rights of patients, healthcare professionals, healthcare facilities, researchers and other individuals whose information may be processed through our platform.

This Notice operates alongside our general Privacy Notice and is guided by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable data protection regulations.

2. What Is Carri Health Data Intelligence?

Carri Health Data Intelligence is a data analytics and health information intelligence service that processes healthcare information generated through participating healthcare facilities.

The service helps transform healthcare data into actionable insights that can support:

  • Healthcare performance monitoring.
  • Disease surveillance and epidemiological analysis.
  • Population health assessment.
  • Healthcare utilisation and service delivery analysis.
  • Health research and medical innovation.
  • Public health planning and policy development.
  • Evidence-based government health interventions.
  • Healthcare resource allocation and management.
  • Identification of healthcare trends and patterns.

Our objective is to make healthcare data more useful for improving health outcomes while maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of individuals.

3. Information We Process

Depending on the services provided and the participating healthcare facility, Carri Health may process the following categories of information for Data Intelligence purposes:

A. Patient and Demographic Information

  • Age and age group.
  • Sex.
  • Geographic location and relevant demographic indicators.
  • Other relevant population characteristics.

B. Clinical and Medical Information

  • Medical diagnoses.
  • Laboratory investigation results.
  • Treatment and medication patterns.
  • Disease prevalence and distribution.
  • Patient admission and discharge information.
  • Healthcare service utilisation.
  • Other relevant clinical information.

C. Healthcare Facility Information

  • Patient attendance statistics.
  • Consultation volumes.
  • Service utilisation records.
  • Facility performance indicators.
  • Operational and administrative data.
  • Healthcare delivery trends.

D. Public Health Information

  • Disease incidence and prevalence.
  • Communicable and non-communicable disease trends.
  • Population health indicators.
  • Regional and community health patterns.
  • Other relevant epidemiological information.

4. How We Use Health Data

Carri Health processes healthcare information for legitimate healthcare intelligence and analytical purposes, including:

1. Generating aggregated healthcare statistics and reports. 2. Identifying disease patterns and emerging health trends. 3. Supporting healthcare facilities in monitoring service delivery. 4. Providing insights for healthcare planning and resource management. 5. Supporting authorised medical and public health research. 6. Assisting relevant public health stakeholders with evidence-based planning. 7. Improving the performance and effectiveness of healthcare information systems. 8. Supporting the development of innovative healthcare solutions.

We do not intend to use Data Intelligence as a means of exposing individual patients or their confidential medical information.

5. Data Anonymisation and De-identification

Patient confidentiality is central to our Data Intelligence operations.

Where healthcare information is used for secondary analytics, research or public health intelligence, Carri Health applies appropriate safeguards, including anonymisation, de-identification, aggregation and other privacy-preserving techniques.

These measures are intended to ensure that analytical reports and insights do not unnecessarily reveal the identity of individual patients.

Where information remains identifiable or can reasonably be linked to an individual, it continues to be treated as personal data and is protected accordingly.

6. Who Can Access Data Intelligence?

Access to Carri Health Data Intelligence services is restricted to authorised users and organisations with legitimate access rights.

These may include:

  • Accredited healthcare facilities.
  • Authorised hospital administrators.
  • Approved healthcare management personnel.
  • Authorised public health institutions.
  • Approved researchers and research institutions.
  • Government health agencies and other authorised stakeholders.

Access is governed by applicable permissions, contractual arrangements, legitimate purposes and relevant data protection requirements.

Carri Health does not provide unrestricted access to patient-level medical records through its Data Intelligence services.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Carri Health may share appropriately anonymised or aggregated health insights with authorised stakeholders for legitimate healthcare, research, planning and public health purposes.

Such stakeholders may include:

  • Government health institutions.
  • Public health agencies.
  • Accredited research institutions.
  • Healthcare development organisations.
  • Approved healthcare partners.

Where identifiable personal data must be shared, such disclosure will only occur where there is an appropriate lawful basis, necessary authorisation and adequate data protection safeguards.

Carri Health does not sell patients' identifiable medical records or disclose confidential patient information for unauthorised commercial purposes.

8. Access Restrictions and Service Environment

Carri Health services are designed to operate within the controlled digital environment of accredited healthcare facilities.

Access to Carri Health services is restricted to authorised users operating within the premises of participating accredited healthcare facilities through the Carri Health server infrastructure.

This access arrangement is intended to strengthen institutional accountability, protect patient information and reduce unauthorised access to healthcare records.

Users are expected to comply with the access permissions, security requirements and confidentiality obligations established by Carri Health and participating healthcare facilities.

9. Data Security and Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect information processed through our Data Intelligence services.

These measures include, as applicable:

  • Access control and user authentication.
  • Role-based access permissions.
  • Data encryption and secure transmission.
  • Secure data storage and management.
  • Monitoring of authorised system activities.
  • Protection against unauthorised access and disclosure.
  • Regular security reviews and system improvements.
  • Appropriate incident detection and response procedures.

We continually work towards strengthening our security infrastructure to maintain the confidentiality, integrity and availability of healthcare information.

10. Data Retention

Carri Health retains information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected or processed, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and operational requirements.

Aggregated and anonymised analytical information may be retained for longer periods where permitted by law and where it can no longer reasonably identify individual data subjects.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Patients and other individuals whose personal information is processed through Carri Health retain their applicable data protection rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

These rights may include:

  • The right to be informed about the processing of personal data.
  • The right to access personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request erasure where applicable.
  • The right to object to certain forms of processing.
  • The right to withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis.
  • The right to data portability where applicable.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Requests relating to patient medical records may be directed through the relevant healthcare facility, while requests concerning Carri Health's own processing activities may be directed to our Data Protection contact.

12. Research and Public Health Use

Carri Health recognises the importance of reliable health information in advancing medical research and improving population health.

Our Data Intelligence services may support authorised research and public health initiatives through appropriately anonymised or aggregated information.

Where a proposed research activity requires identifiable personal data, additional legal, ethical and institutional approvals may be required before processing takes place.

Carri Health does not authorise the use of patient information for unrelated research or secondary purposes without an appropriate lawful basis and necessary safeguards.

13. Third-Party Services

Carri Health may engage carefully selected third-party service providers to support its infrastructure, data processing, cloud services, cybersecurity and analytics operations.

Such providers are required to operate within applicable contractual, confidentiality and data protection obligations.

Where third-party involvement requires access to personal data, appropriate safeguards and authorisation will be established.

14. Changes to This Notice

Carri Health may update this Privacy Notice periodically to reflect changes in our Data Intelligence services, technology, operational practices or applicable legal requirements.

Any material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels.

We encourage users and stakeholders to review this Notice periodically.
